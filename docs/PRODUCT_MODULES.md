# Product modules

CredOS is built around tenant-scoped receivables operations. The initial release delivers identity, company, customer, invoice, payment, dashboard, and collection workflows. The remaining modules below are deliberately scaffolded now so their APIs and data ownership do not become accidental later.

| Area | Core records | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communications | templates, deliveries, callbacks | compliant, measurable reminders |
| Risk intelligence | policies, feature snapshots, score runs | explainable credit decisions |
| Trust network | consent, dispute, reputation events | controlled inter-company trust signals |
| Reporting | report runs, exports, snapshots | reliable financial and operational visibility |
| Integrations | connections, sync mappings, webhook deliveries | safe ERP and partner connectivity |

No trust-sharing or automated adverse credit action may be enabled without documented consent, human review, retention rules, and legal/compliance sign-off.
